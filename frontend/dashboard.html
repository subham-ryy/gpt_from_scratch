Training Journey

10:24 PM

This model is a 98.4 million parameter GPT that was built and trained from scratch through three main stages:

  1. 1
    Pretraining – learned general language patterns from about 600 million tokens of high-quality educational web data.
  2. 2
    Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT) – trained on curated conversational and instruction datasets to behave like a helpful assistant.
  3. 3
    Direct Preference Optimization (DPO) – further aligned the model using preference pairs to reduce rambling, repetition and improve answer quality.

Is it as smart as the big models? No. Did I train it myself on a laptop that sounded like a jet engine? Absolutely. Treat it like a very confident intern who's still learning.
